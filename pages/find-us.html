De Waterkant Flagship

Situated in the industrial heart of De Waterkant, our flagship space occupies a restored warehouse that mirrors our commitment to structural honesty. The interior utilizes raw concrete and modular steel to create a focused environment for coffee exploration. It is a space designed for the intersection of precision brewing and quiet study—a physical manifestation of the Nerv ethos within Cape Town’s original coffee district.

Address: 123 Waterkant Street, Cape Town

Hours: Mon-Fri 7am-7pm, Sat-Sun 8am-6pm

Contact: +27 21 123 4567 |
